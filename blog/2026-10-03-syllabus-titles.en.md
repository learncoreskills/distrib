# Syllabus rows now have a title and a description

Every skill in the Mathematics syllabus used to be one long sentence. Now each row has a short
heading and, under it, the sentence that explains it. For the first pre-school skill, a parent sees
**Know the numbers 1 to 10**, then "Recites number words 1–10 in order, without skipping or
repeating." In French: **Connaître les nombres de 1 à 10**, then "Récite la comptine numérique de 1 à
10 dans l'ordre, sans sauter ni répéter de nombre."

That row also gets a second activity. The old *Count in Order* is split in two: **Count in Order
(1-5)** for the little ones, and **Count in Order (1-10)** for the next step. Both practise the same
skill, so progress on it counts either way.

Titles exist for every grade from pre-school to Grade 5, in English and French.
