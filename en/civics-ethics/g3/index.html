Skills

  • Knows what taxes are, in simple terms, and what they pay for.
  • Knows children have rights: to be safe, schooled, heard, cared for, and free from harm.
  • Knows what a stereotype is and can spot a simple one.
  • Knows that a disabled person may need a different kind of access, not a different kind of respect.
  • Distinguishes an accident from something done on purpose.
  • Apologises for the harm caused, not merely to end the conversation.
