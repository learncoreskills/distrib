Skills

  • Value of each digit in 4-digit numbers Says the 3 in 4,306 is worth 300 and the 4 is worth 4,000.
  • Partition 4-digit numbers; zero as placeholder Writes 4,306 as 4,000 + 300 + 6 and says what the 0 in the tens place is doing.
  • Add or subtract 1, 10, 100, 1000 Says 10 more than 4,396 is 4,406 and 1000 less than 5,090 is 4,090 without a written sum.
  • Round to the nearest 10 Rounds 4,376 to 4,380 and 2,455 to 2,460, deciding by the ones digit.
  • Round to the nearest 100 Rounds 4,376 to 4,400 and 2,450 to 2,500, deciding by the tens digit.
  • Multiply by 10 and 100 by shifting digits Explains 35 × 100 = 3,500 as each digit moving two places left, with zeros holding the empty places.
  • Read a 4-digit number with blocks Reads 2,345 as 2 thousands, 3 hundreds, 4 tens, 5 ones with blocks. Moved from G2.
