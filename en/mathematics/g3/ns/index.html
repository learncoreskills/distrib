Skills

  • Read and write numbers to 10,000 Reads 6,205 aloud and writes "four thousand and nine" as 4,009, handling zeros inside the number.
  • Compare and order 4-digit numbers Puts 5,342; 5,324; 4,999 in order and says which is greater by comparing thousands first, then hundreds.
  • Place a number on a line to 10,000 Puts 6,250 on a line labelled 0 and 10,000 by working out which thousand it lies between and roughly where in it.
  • Read unlabelled marks on a zoomed line Reads the number at an unlabelled mark on a line from 6,000 to 7,000 split into tens of hundreds (the mark is 6,400).
  • Count in 4s, 6s, 7s, 8s and 9s Counts forward and back in 4s, 6s, 7s, 8s and 9s from any multiple (42, 49, 56, 63, 72).
  • Count in 25s, 50s, 100s and 1000s Counts 25, 50, 75, 100, 125, in 50s and 100s (350, 400, 450), and 3,000, 4,000, 5,000, and says the next number after 975.
  • Roman numerals to C (non-gating) Reads XXXIV as 34 and writes 49 as XLIX using I, V, X, L, C. Local extra.
  • Compare temperatures below zero (introduce) Says −3 °C is colder than −1 °C and orders −4, −1, 2 on a vertical thermometer. Explore only.
  • Count back through zero (introduce) Counts 3, 2, 1, 0, −1, −2 and says which floor is two below the ground floor. Explore only.
