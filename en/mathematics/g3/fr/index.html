Skills

  • Count in tenths Counts 1/10, 2/10, … 10/10 and past, knowing tenths come from cutting a whole in ten.
  • Count in hundredths Counts 1/100, 2/100 … and says ten hundredths make one tenth (introduce).
  • Fractions on a 0–1 line and past 1 Places 3/4 and 5/4 on a line from 0 to 2, seeing fractions as numbers with a position.
  • Equivalent fractions on a fraction wall Shows ½ = 2/4 = 4/8 by lining up strips, models only.
  • Tenths equal hundredths Shows 3/10 = 30/100 on a 100-square by shading three columns (models only, no simplifying).
  • Compare same-denominator and same-numerator fractions Says 3/8 > 2/8 (same size parts, more of them) and 1/3 > 1/5 (fewer cuts, bigger parts), and justifies with a model.
  • Add same-denominator fractions Works out 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8 with a bar.
  • Add beyond one whole Works out 5/8 + 7/8 = 12/8 and sees it is more than one whole. No conversion rule yet. G4 preview, going beyond G3.FR.6 "within 1".
  • Subtract same-denominator fractions Works out 7/8 − 5/8 = 2/8 and 7/10 − 3/10 = 4/10. G4.FR.4 preview only: 12/8 − 5/8 = 7/8.
  • Fraction of a quantity Finds ¼ of 20 = 5 and ¾ of 20 = 15, and solves a simple problem with them.
  • Name a/b as a parts of size 1/b Says 3/4 is three pieces each worth 1/4, for denominators 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 10.
  • Whole numbers as fractions Says 3 = 3/1 and 4/4 = 1, and that 6/3 sits on the same spot as 2.
