Skills

  • Converting units of time Converts 3 weeks to days and 2 days to hours.
  • Decimal and mixed time units Converts 2.5 h = 150 min and 135 min = 2 h 15 min.
  • Reading timetables and finding durations Finds that a 08:45 bus arriving 10:20 takes 1 h 35 min.
  • Multi-leg journeys across midnight Plans a trip with two legs and a wait and an arrival after midnight.
  • Time in another time zone (introduce) Says that when it is 10:00 in Paris it is 09:00 in London.
  • Scheduling across time zones (introduce) Picks a call time that is a sensible hour in two cities.
