Skills

  • Converting fractions, decimals and percent Converts ⅛ = 0.125 = 12.5% by dividing, reads percent as "out of 100" on a 100-square (37% = 37/100 = 0.37) and moves between forms for common values.
  • Ordering a mix of fractions, decimals and percent Orders ⅗, 0.58 and 61% from smallest to largest.
  • Percent of an amount Finds 15% of 80 = 12 as 10% + 5%.
  • Percentage increase and decrease Works out a 25% discount on €40 (€30) and a 10% rise on 60 (66).
  • What percent one quantity is of another Says 12 of 48 is 25%.
  • Finding the whole from a percentage Works out that 45 is 30% of 150.
  • Ratio notation and simplifying Writes 6 red to 4 blue as 6:4 = 3:2 and writes a:b:c for three parts.
  • Sharing a quantity in a ratio Shares 20 in the ratio 3:1 (15 and 5).
  • Proportion by unit value and scaling Solves "3 cost 12, what do 7 cost?" (28).
  • Unit rates and speed Finds that 150 km in 3 h is 50 km/h and uses it to predict a distance.
  • Converting units by ratio reasoning Converts 72 km/h to 20 m/s and 250 cm to km.
