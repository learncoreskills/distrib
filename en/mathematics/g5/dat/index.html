Skills

  • Mode and range Finds mode 5 and range 7 in 2, 5, 5, 9, 3.
  • Mean Shares out to level the data and calculates the mean (3, 5, 7 gives 5).
  • When the mean misleads Spots that one extreme value drags the mean (1, 2, 2, 3, 40).
  • Median Finds the median of 4, 9, 2, 7, 5 by ordering (5).
  • Reading a pie chart Reads that a quarter-sized sector is 25% and ¼ of the total.
  • Drawing a pie chart (introduce) Turns 50%, 30%, 20% into a pie.
  • Line graphs with two series Reads two lines on one graph and estimates a value between two points.
  • Choosing the right graph Picks a bar chart, line graph or pie chart to suit the data.
  • Spotting a misleading graph Notices a truncated axis, a missing scale or a biased sample.
