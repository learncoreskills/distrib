Skills

  • Shape hierarchy Says every square is a rectangle and every rectangle is a parallelogram.
  • Properties that define quadrilaterals Tells a kite, a rhombus and a trapezium apart by sides, parallels and diagonals.
  • Parts of a circle Names centre, radius, diameter and circumference.
  • Radius and diameter relationship Knows d = 2r, so a circle of radius 4 cm has diameter 8 cm.
  • Angles in a triangle Finds the missing angle in a triangle with 50° and 60° (70°).
  • Angles in a quadrilateral Finds the missing angle in a quadrilateral with 90°, 80° and 100° (90°).
  • Vertically opposite angles Knows that when two lines cross, opposite angles are equal (a 65° angle faces another 65°).
  • Drawing a shape from a specification Draws a triangle with sides 5 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm using ruler, protractor and compasses.
  • Nets of solids Tells which unfolded shapes fold into a cube, cuboid, prism or pyramid.
  • Top, front and side views Matches a solid to its top, front and side views.
