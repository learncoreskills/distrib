Skills

  • Mental 2-digit × 1-digit Works out 48 × 6 by splitting into 40 × 6 + 8 × 6.
  • Multiples of 10 and 100 mentally Calculates 70 × 300 = 21,000 from 7 × 3.
  • Simple decimals mentally Calculates 0.6 + 0.7 = 1.3 and 2.5 × 4 = 10 in the head.
  • Deriving new facts from known ones Gets 4 × 0.3 = 1.2 from 4 × 3 = 12 and ⅜ of 24 from ⅛ of 24.
  • Common percentages of an amount Finds 50%, 10% and 1% of 360 in the head (180, 36, 3.6).
  • 25% and 75% by halving and quartering Finds 25% of 80 as half of half, and 75% as 3 quarters.
  • Estimating before calculating Rounds 4.8 × 52 to 5 × 50 and expects about 250 before working it out.
  • Spotting answers that are out by a factor of ten Knows that 4.8 × 52 cannot be 24.96 or 2,496 by checking the magnitude.
