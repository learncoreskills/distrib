Skills

  • Finding a common denominator Rewrites ⅔ and ¾ as 8/12 and 9/12.
  • Adding and subtracting unlike fractions Works out ⅔ + ¾ = 1 5/12 and ⅚ − ¼ = 7/12.
  • Adding and subtracting mixed numbers Works out 3 ½ − 1 ¾ = 1 ¾ by regrouping.
  • Comparing and ordering fractions above 1 Orders 7/4, 1 ½ and 5/3 and 2 ⅙.
  • Fraction as division Reads ¾ as 3 ÷ 4 and writes 5 ÷ 2 as 2 ½.
  • A fraction times a whole number Works out ⅗ × 20 = 12 and 4 × ⅔ = 2 ⅔.
  • Fraction × fraction with an area model Shades ⅔ of ¾ on a grid to see ⅔ × ¾ = ½ and knows the result is smaller than either.
  • Unit fraction ÷ whole, whole ÷ unit fraction Works out ⅓ ÷ 2 = ⅙ and 4 ÷ ⅓ = 12.
  • Fraction ÷ fraction (introduce) Sees ¾ ÷ ¼ = 3 and 4 ÷ ⅔ = 6 by counting how many fit.
  • Finding the whole from a part Works out ⅗ of ? = 30 is 50.
  • Simplifying fractions Simplifies 12/18 to ⅔ by dividing top and bottom by the common factor 6.
