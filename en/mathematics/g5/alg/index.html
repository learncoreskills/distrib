Skills

  • Writing expressions with a letter Writes n + 5 for "5 more than a number" and 3 × n for "3 times a number".
  • Substituting into a formula Uses P = 2 × (l + w) with l = 7, w = 4 (22).
  • One-step equations and checking Solves n + 7 = 15 and 4 × n = 36 and checks by substituting back.
  • Simple two-step equations Solves 2n + 3 = 13 by undoing the steps (n = 5).
  • Pairs that satisfy an equation Finds pairs with a + b = 10 and a > b (9 and 1, 8 and 2, ...).
  • Stating a sequence rule by position Says "4 × position + 1" for 5, 9, 13, 17.
  • Using a rule to predict far terms Uses the rule to find the 50th term (201).
  • Two patterns from two rules Builds two sequences (add 2 from 0, add 4 from 0) and pairs the terms as (2, 4), (4, 8).
  • Relationships between paired patterns Sees each term in one pattern is twice its partner and says so.
