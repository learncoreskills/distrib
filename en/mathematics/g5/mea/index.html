Skills

  • Converting metric units with decimals Converts 2.35 km = 2,350 m and 450 ml = 0.45 l.
  • Choosing units and two-step conversions Chooses km, m or cm for a job and converts mm to m through cm.
  • Rough customary equivalents (non-gating) Knows an inch is about 2.5 cm, a foot about 30 cm, a mile about 1.6 km, a pound about 450 g and a pint about 570 ml.
  • Personal benchmarks Uses own hand span and pace to estimate a room (about 6 paces) and own height to estimate a door or a shelf.
  • Area of a parallelogram Finds the area of a parallelogram as base × height (6 × 4 = 24).
  • Area of a triangle Finds the area of a triangle as half of a base × height rectangle.
  • Area and perimeter of compound rectilinear shapes Finds the area and perimeter of an L-shape by splitting it into rectangles.
  • Volume of cuboids and compound cuboids Works out V = l × w × h in cm³ and m³ (4 × 3 × 2 = 24 cm³), including two blocks joined.
  • Volume and capacity: ml and cm³ Knows 1 ml = 1 cm³ and 1 l = 1,000 cm³ (a 10 cm cube holds 1 l).
