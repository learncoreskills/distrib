Skills

  • Multi-digit multiplication by a written method Multiplies 3,482 × 26 with the standard algorithm and checks by estimate.
  • Multiplying and dividing decimals by whole numbers Works out 3.45 × 6 and 7.2 ÷ 4 with a written method.
  • Decimal × decimal Uses scaling to see 0.3 × 0.4 = 0.12 (3 × 4 = 12, then ÷ 100).
  • Long division by a 2-digit number Divides 925 ÷ 25 with a written method.
  • Expressing the remainder to fit the story Gives 50 ÷ 8 as 6 r 2, 6 ¼ or 6.25, whichever the story needs.
  • Decimal ÷ decimal (introduce) Scales both numbers by 10 so 1.2 ÷ 0.3 becomes 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
  • Order of operations Evaluates 2 + 3 × 4 = 14 and 18 − 6 ÷ 3 = 16.
  • Brackets and exponents in expressions Evaluates 2 + 3² × 4 and (2 + 3)² × 4 and sees the brackets change the answer; writes a bracketed expression for a story (3 × (4 + 5)).
  • Choosing a method Picks mental, written or calculator for 4,000 − 1,998 vs 6,284 ÷ 17 and checks against an estimate.
  • Column addition and subtraction of whole and decimal numbers Works out 12.5 + 3.482 and 20 − 7.35 in columns, lining up the decimal point.
