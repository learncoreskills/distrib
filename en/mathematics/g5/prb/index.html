Skills

  • Language of chance and the 0–1 scale Places "unlikely" near 0.2 and "certain" at 1 on a scale.
  • Simple probabilities as fractions Writes the chance of a 5 on a die as 1/6 and a red marble from 3 red in 10 as 3/10.
  • Fair and unfair Says a spinner with unequal sectors is unfair and designs a fair one.
  • Experiments against predictions Predicts 25 heads in 50 tosses, runs it and compares.
  • More trials get closer to the prediction Sees that 1,000 rolls match the prediction better than 10 rolls.
