Skills

  • Reading and writing numbers into the billions Reads and writes a number in words and digits with the right place groupings (8,431,000 is eight million four hundred thirty-one thousand).
  • Feeling how big a billion is Explains that a billion is a thousand millions by building it from stacked thousands and millions.
  • Comparing and ordering large numbers Orders 4,205,000, 4,250,000 and 420,500 by looking at the highest differing place.
  • Rounding large numbers in a story Rounds a number to a sensible place for the story (8,431,000 people is about 8 million).
  • Negative numbers: order and absolute value Orders −7, 2, −3, 0 and says that −4 is 4 away from zero (absolute value).
  • Adding and subtracting integers on a number line Works out −3 + 5 and 2 − 6 by hopping on a line.
  • One number line for every kind of number Places 0.7, ¾, −1.5 and 3 together on one line in the right spots.
