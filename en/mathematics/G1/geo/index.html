Skills

  • Name 2D shapes Names circle, triangle, square, rectangle, pentagon and hexagon. Example: "that is a hexagon".
  • Shapes in any size and orientation Knows a shape stays the same when turned or resized. Example: a tilted triangle is still a triangle.
  • What makes a triangle a triangle Says it is closed with 3 straight sides, and colour and size do not matter. Example: rejects a shape with a curved side.
  • Name 3D solids Names cube, cuboid, sphere, cylinder, cone and pyramid. Example: picks the cone from a bag.
  • Link solids to everyday objects Matches solids to things around us. Example: a can is a cylinder.
  • Build composite shapes Builds a bigger shape from others, in 2D and from 3D blocks. Example: a house from a square and a triangle, or a tower model from cubes and cuboids.
  • Copy a figure on a grid Copies a figure on square or dot paper. Example: copies a small boat.
  • Straight and curved lines Tells straight from curved lines. Example: a road bend is curved.
  • Draw a straight line with a ruler Joins two points with a straight line. Example: draws between two dots.
