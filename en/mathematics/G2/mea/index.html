Skills

  • Choose the tool and unit for length Picks a ruler for a pencil in cm and a tape for a room in m.
  • Measure to the nearest cm Lines up a pencil at 0 and reads 14 cm; with a metre stick or tape measures a room or door in m.
  • Estimate lengths and check Guesses 30 cm for a book, then measures.
  • How much longer Finds a 45 cm ribbon is 18 cm longer than a 27 cm one.
  • Lengths and differences on a number line Shows 12 cm and 20 cm on a line and the 8 cm gap.
  • Read kg and g scales Reads a scale to the nearest labelled mark: 2 kg, 500 g.
  • Read l and ml measures Reads a jug to the nearest labelled ml: 300 ml, 2 l.
  • Pick a sensible unit and the metric links Chooses kg for flour, ml for a spoon of medicine, and knows 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 l = 1000 ml.
  • Read a Celsius thermometer Reads 23 °C and says if it is hot or cold.
