Skills

  • Bridge through 10 when adding 1-digit numbers Adds 8 to 47 by 47 + 3 = 50, + 5 = 55.
  • Subtract a 1-digit number crossing a ten Works 52 − 6 as 52 − 2 = 50, − 4 = 46.
  • Add and subtract tens mentally Does 46 + 30 = 76 and 90 − 40 = 50 in their head.
  • Derived facts: scale a known fact Uses 3 + 7 = 10 to know 30 + 70 = 100, and 8 − 5 = 3 to know 80 − 50 = 30.
  • Mental ± of hundreds, tens and ones in 3-digit numbers Does 472 + 300 = 772, 472 + 20 = 492, 472 + 5 = 477 without writing.
  • Doubles to 50 Doubles 17 to 34 and 25 to 50 by doubling tens and ones.
  • Halves of even numbers to 100 Halves 86 to 43 by halving tens and ones.
  • Round to the nearest 10 on a number line Says 38 is nearer 40 than 30, and 35 rounds up.
  • Estimate a sum or difference by rounding Says 38 + 53 is about 90 and 83 − 41 is about 40.
  • Estimate a simple product by rounding Says 4 × 19 is about 80 by using 4 × 20 (introduce-only).
  • Check answers by estimating and by the inverse Checks 63 + 29 = 92 by estimating (60 + 30) and by 92 − 29.
