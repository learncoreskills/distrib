Skills

  • Sides and vertices of polygons Names triangle, quadrilateral, pentagon, hexagon, octagon from 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 sides and vertices.
  • Compare properties of 2D shapes Says a square and a rectangle both have 4 sides and 4 square corners.
  • Faces, edges and vertices of 3D shapes Counts 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices on a cube and names the square faces.
  • Sort shapes by properties Sorts 2D shapes into "has a square corner" and "has 5 sides" groups, then sorts 3D solids (flat faces, curved surface, can roll).
  • Draw shapes with given attributes Draws a 5-sided shape on a dot grid with a ruler.
  • Line of symmetry by folding Finds the vertical fold that makes two halves match.
  • Right angles Spots square corners with a paper set square.
  • Draw and measure a line segment Draws a 7 cm segment and measures a given one.
  • Are three points on a straight line? Lays a ruler to check whether A, B, C line up.
  • Rows and columns of squares Splits a 3-by-4 rectangle into 12 equal squares.
