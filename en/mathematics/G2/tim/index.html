Skills

  • Tell time to the quarter hour Reads o'clock, half past, quarter past and quarter to.
  • Tell time to 5 minutes Reads 3:25 and 4:50 by counting in fives round the clock.
  • Write time and a.m./p.m. Writes 4:35 p.m. and says breakfast is a.m.
  • Time units: minutes, hours, days, weeks, months Knows 60 min, 24 h, 7 days, 12 months.
  • Days in each month Knows 30 days hath September and that February is the short one.
  • Duration in whole hours Says 3:00 to 5:00 is 2 hours.
  • Duration in half hours Works out 3:00 to 5:30 is 2 and a half hours.
