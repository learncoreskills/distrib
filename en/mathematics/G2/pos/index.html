Skills

  • Quarter and half turns Turns a robot a quarter or a half turn.
  • Three-quarter turns, clockwise and anticlockwise Says "three-quarter turn anticlockwise".
  • Read a grid reference Finds cell B4.
  • Place and name positions on a grid Puts the flag on D2 and gives the reference of any object.
  • Write moves to reach a target Writes "right 3, up 2" to take a token to a target.
  • Routes with turns, and fixing a route Combines "forward 3, turn right, forward 2" and finds the faulty step.
