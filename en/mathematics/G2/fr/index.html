Skills

  • Unit fractions: 1/b of a whole Names 1/4 as one of 4 equal parts and 1/6 as one of 6, denominators 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10; knows equal shares of identical wholes need not look alike.
  • Non-unit fractions a/b Shows 3/4 as 3 of 4 equal parts (3 pieces of 1/4).
  • Fractions on the 0–1 number line Places 3/4 and 3/8 on a 0–1 line cut into equal parts.
  • Fractions beyond 1 and whole numbers as fractions Knows 4/4 = 1, 3 = 3/1 and finds 5/4 just past 1.
  • Count in halves and quarters Counts ¼, ½, ¾, 1, 1¼… and ½, 1, 1½, 2… along a number line, forwards and back.
  • Equivalent fractions with models Shows with folding that 2/4 = 1/2 and 3/6 = 1/2. Models only, no rule.
  • Compare fractions with the same denominator Says 3/8 < 5/8 and explains "more eighths".
  • Compare fractions with the same numerator Says 1/3 > 1/5 and explains "smaller parts".
  • Count in tenths Counts 0.1 steps as 1/10, 2/10, 3/10… up to 10/10 and knows tenths come from dividing by 10.
  • Add like fractions within one whole (models only) Shows 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7 with strips and says "the sevenths stay"; takes 2/7 from 5/7 to leave 3/7.
  • Find 1/4 and 3/4 of 20, and simple fractions of 6 and 12 Finds 1/4 of 20 = 5 by sharing in 4 groups and 3/4 = 15; finds 1/3 of 6 = 2, 1/4 of 12 = 3 and 3/4 of 12 = 9 with objects.
