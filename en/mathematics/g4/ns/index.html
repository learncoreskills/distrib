Skills

  • Read and write numbers to the millions Reads and writes 3,070,400 as "three million, seventy thousand, four hundred" and back.
  • Compare and order numbers to the millions Decides that 4,206,090 is more than 4,196,999 by comparing from the highest place and orders a set of 4–5 such numbers.
  • Read billions by 3-digit groups (enrichment, non-gating) Reads 2,340,000,000 as "two billion, three hundred forty million" by reading each group of three.
  • Place big numbers on a number line Places 640,000 on a 0–1,000,000 line and finds the midpoint of 2,000,000 and 3,000,000 (the billions part is enrichment, non-gating; G4.NUM.1 covers only the to-1,000,000 part).
  • Roman numerals to M (local, non-gating) Reads XIV, XLIX and MCMXC and the year on a monument.
  • Lakh/crore commas (local, non-gating) Reads 12,50,000 as "twelve lakh fifty thousand" and knows it equals 1,250,000.
  • Count back through zero Counts 3, 1, −1, −3 on a number line and says what comes after −2 going up.
  • Gap between a negative and a positive number Finds the gap between −4 °C and 7 °C (11 degrees) or between floor −2 and floor 3 (5 floors).
