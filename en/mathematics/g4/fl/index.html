Skills

  • Multiply and divide multiples of 10 and 100 mentally Computes 30 × 40 = 1,200 and 2,400 ÷ 6 = 400 from the basic fact.
  • Add and subtract by rounding and compensating Computes 346 + 99 as 346 + 100 − 1 and 502 − 298 as 502 − 300 + 2.
  • Double and halve to simplify Turns 16 × 25 into 8 × 50 into 4 × 100 = 400.
  • Estimate products Estimates 48 × 6 as 50 × 6 = 300 and decides whether 288 is reasonable.
  • Estimate quotients with friendly numbers Estimates 412 ÷ 8 as 400 ÷ 8 = 50 using compatible numbers.
  • Choose the best mental strategy Picks compensation for 346 + 99, double-and-halve for 16 × 25 and tens-facts for 30 × 40.
  • Recall facts to 12×12 and matching divisions Recalls 7 × 8 = 56, 12 × 11 = 132 and 132 ÷ 12 = 11 instantly.
