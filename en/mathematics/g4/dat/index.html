Skills

  • Draw and read bar charts with a scale Draws a bar chart with a scale of 2 per square and reads a bar that ends between labels.
  • Double bar charts Compares boys and girls on a double bar chart ("5 more preferred swimming").
  • Draw and read line graphs Plots temperature at each hour and reads the value between two marks.
  • Describe the trend of a line graph Says "it rose until noon, then fell" and which period changed fastest.
  • Read and complete two-way tables Fills in a two-way table (class × favourite sport) and answers "how many girls chose football?".
  • Line plots with fractions of a unit Plots pencil lengths in ¼ cm on a line plot and finds the difference between the longest and shortest.
  • Pose a question and collect data Rewrites "Do you like sport?" as "Which sport do you like best?" and tallies answers.
  • Pick the graph that fits the data Chooses a line graph for temperature over a week and a bar chart for favourite fruits.
