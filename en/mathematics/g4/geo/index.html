Skills

  • Points, lines, segments and rays Tells a line segment from a ray and a line and names them (AB, ray CD).
  • Parallel and perpendicular lines Finds the parallel and perpendicular pairs on a map or a letter shape and draws them.
  • Name angle types Calls 45° acute, 90° right, 120° obtuse, 180° straight and 250° reflex.
  • Measure and draw angles with a protractor Reads 65° from the correct scale and draws a 110° angle.
  • Angles on a straight line and around a point Finds the missing angle as 180° − 125° = 55° on a line and 360° − 270° = 90° around a point.
  • Classify triangles by sides Names a triangle with sides 5, 5 and 8 as isosceles.
  • Classify triangles by angles Names a triangle with a 100° angle obtuse and one with a 90° angle right.
  • Regular and irregular polygons Explains that a rhombus is irregular as its angles are not equal, while a square is regular.
  • Find lines of symmetry Finds all 4 lines of symmetry of a square and the single line of an isosceles triangle.
  • Complete a symmetric figure Draws the mirror half of a figure across a vertical, horizontal or diagonal line.
  • Parts of a circle and drawing with compasses Names centre, radius and diameter (diameter = 2 × radius) and draws a circle of radius 3 cm.
  • Faces, edges and vertices of prisms and pyramids Says a triangular prism has 5 faces, 9 edges and 6 vertices and a square pyramid has 5, 8 and 5.
