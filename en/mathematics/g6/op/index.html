Skills

  • Order of operations with negatives Evaluates with negatives and brackets (−3 × (4 + 5) = −27; 2 − 3 × −4 = 14).
  • Powers and roots inside expressions Evaluates with squares, cubes and roots in the order (2 + 3² = 11; √16 − 2³ = −4).
  • Area model of column multiplication Explains 23 × 14 as four pieces on an area model and links it to the written method.
  • The distributive law Uses a(b + c) = ab + ac to calculate (7 × 98 = 7 × 100 − 7 × 2) and to explain mental tricks.
  • Area model of products of binomials Shows (x + 3)(x + 2) as four area pieces (introduce only).
  • Multiplying powers with the same base Applies aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ with small whole-number powers (2³ × 2⁴ = 2⁷; introduce only).
