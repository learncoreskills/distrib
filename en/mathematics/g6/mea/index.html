Skills

  • Parts of a circle and where π comes from Names radius, diameter and circumference, and finds the ratio C ÷ d ≈ 3.14 by rolling.
  • Circumference Uses C = πd and C = 2πr (d = 10 cm gives about 31.4 cm).
  • Area of a circle Uses A = πr² and tells r² from 2r (r = 3 gives about 28.3).
  • Comparing circles Compares circle sizes in context (is one 16 cm pizza bigger than two 10 cm ones?).
  • Volume of cuboids as layers Computes volume as base area × height by layers (3 × 4 × 5 = 60), and recognises a prism as a solid with a constant cross-section, naming that cross-section.
  • Volume of triangular prisms Computes triangular-prism volume as triangle area × length.
  • Volume of circular prisms (cylinder idea) Computes V = πr²h as base area × height.
  • Speed, distance and time Computes speed = distance ÷ time and rearranges (120 km in 2 h is 60 km/h; 60 km/h for 3 h is 180 km).
  • Converting compound units and density-style rates Converts km/h ↔ m/s (72 km/h = 20 m/s), uses g per cm³, keeps units.
