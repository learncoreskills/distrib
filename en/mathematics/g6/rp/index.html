Skills

  • Percent as a multiplier Uses ×1.2 for +20% and ×0.85 for −15%, including above 100% (130% of 40 = 52).
  • Repeated percentage change Chains multipliers (two 10% rises make ×1.21; +10% then −10% is not back to start).
  • Reverse percentages Finds the original by dividing by the multiplier (after 20% off it is 48, so 60).
  • Percent error Expresses an error as a percent of the true value (guessed 45, actual 50, error 10%). Pure arithmetic only.
  • Ratios as 1 : n and equivalent ratios Rewrites 4 : 10 as 1 : 2.5 and compares ratios.
  • Sharing in three-part ratios Shares a quantity in a 3-part ratio (£60 as pure number in 2 : 3 : 5 = 12, 18, 30), with mixed units.
  • Direct proportion and the constant k Uses y = kx and finds k from one pair (3 books cost 12, so k = 4).
  • Inverse proportion Recognises that more workers mean fewer days (xy constant: 6 workers take 4 days, so 3 take 8).
  • Additive vs multiplicative reasoning Tells "add the same amount" from "scale by the same factor" (a recipe for 2 scaled to 5 by adding 3 is wrong).
