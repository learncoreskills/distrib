The progress report now opens up, level by level

The parent page's progress report used to give one overall percentage and two short lists, which told you how things stood but not where to look. Now each subject opens into its topics, each topic into its skills, and each skill into the activities your child actually played, with a percentage at every level.

It starts compact: the subject, its score, and its topics. Tap a topic to see its skills, tap a skill to see each activity's own score, or "not played yet" for one that has not been tried.

Mathematics            62%
  ▾ Numbers            71%
      ▾ Count a set    80%
          Dot counting      90%
          Number line       not played yet
  ▸ Fractions          40%

Two buttons, Expand all and Collapse all, open or close everything at once, and every row also opens on its own, so you can follow one topic without scrolling through the rest. The numbers are the same ones your child sees in the syllabus, available in English and French. Only skills the app can score are listed, and while no grade is set it shows all grades.
